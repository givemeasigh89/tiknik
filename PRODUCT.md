## Platform

web

## Users

Confirmed by the atelier (November 2026). Three groups, all primary:

- **Brides in Armenia** who come to the Yerevan atelier and are fitted in person.
- **Brides abroad** (the Armenian diaspora and others) who order remotely: measurements, photos and videos are shared at every stage, and the dress is shipped.
- **Women renting a piece** for a pre-wedding photoshoot, an engagement party or a celebration.

Their job on the site: find a piece or the idea of one, trust that it is made by hand for them, and start a conversation (cart request or fitting request).

## Product Purpose

Tiknik makes beautiful handmade dresses in Yerevan, not only for weddings: to buy (sewn to the client's measurements) or to rent for events, photoshoots and celebrations. Bridal is a core part, not the whole. The site shows the collection, explains how a dress is made, and turns interest into a request the atelier answers personally.

## Positioning

What Tiknik can say that a neighbouring atelier cannot truthfully copy (confirmed):

- **One dress, one bride.** Dresses are almost always made to order, to one bride's measurements. Of any
  model only one piece exists at a time; another is sewn only for a new order.
- **Always handmade**, in a home sewing studio in Yerevan (not a shop floor).
- **Vintage lace when it fits.** Original vintage lace is used when it is available and the bride wants it;
  other pieces use new lace. So "vintage lace" must not be claimed for every dress.

Supporting story (from the atelier's own About text): the name comes from the founder's childhood title *Kendani Tiknik*, the Living Doll; a dress is meant to become a family heirloom.

## Brand commitments

- **Three languages, always:** English, Russian and Armenian. Every visible text exists in all three.
- **The key logo and the TIKNIK wordmark** stay as they are.

## Current mechanism (facts from the site, not brand commitments)

- No online payment: the cart sends a request, and size, dates and payment are agreed personally.
- Delivery: free collection or local delivery in Armenia; worldwide by registered post, 10–30 days.
- The site runs on Flask on PythonAnywhere; content is edited in `/admin`.

## Design reference

The atelier likes https://www.lelarose.com/ as overall inspiration (November 2026). Inspiration only: nothing is copied.
